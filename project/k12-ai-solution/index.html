02 · 政策背景与市场趋势

政策驱动明确，市场已在爆发

作业管理是"双减"落地的关键抓手，也是教育数字化最容易量化的切口——政策、市场与技术三条曲线在同一时间点交汇。

02

教育数字化

党的二十大报告首次写入"推进教育数字化"；《教育强国建设规划纲要（2024—2035 年）》部署实施教育数字化战略，促进人工智能助力教育变革。

03

"AI + 教育"

教育部《关于加强中小学人工智能教育的通知》（2024）提出 2030 年前基本普及中小学人工智能教育；《新一代人工智能发展规划》明确"智能教育"方向。

中国 AI 学习硬件 2024 年出货量约 1,046 万台（艾媒咨询）；"学练机"等新品类 2024 年市场规模约 15.5 亿元，"人工智能+"行动加速教育智能硬件渗透。

02 · Market & policy

Why Europe, why now

Three curves cross at the same moment: a regulatory window that rewards compliance-by-design, a market growing faster than procurement can absorb, and a teaching workforce that cannot absorb more admin.

EU

Policy window

EU Digital Education Action Plan 2021–2027; the AI Act classifies education AI as high-risk, with core obligations from August 2026 — compliant-by-design tools win procurement.

↗

Global AI-in-education market value: USD 6B (2024) → USD 32.3B (2030), 31.2% CAGR (Grand View Research). Europe accounts for roughly one third of worldwide EdTech spend.

↓

Real pain

PISA 2022: EU maths fell 15 points vs 2018 — the steepest drop on record. Teachers work 38.8 h/week, about one third of it on non-teaching tasks (OECD TALIS).

☐

UNESCO warns about classroom phone distraction; around 25% of systems now restrict phones in class, up from 16% in 2023. Finland legislated a ban in 2025. Outcomes — without more screens.

05 · 产品线 B

AI 个性化学习引擎 —— 策略可审计的因材施教

不是"多做题"，而是把每一道题用在最该用的地方：会的不重复，不会的反复变着法练。

有科学依据，也有数据主权

05 · Product line B

AI Personalized Practice Engine — pedagogy you can audit

Not "more practice" — the right item in the right place. Nothing already mastered is repeated; everything unmastered comes back in a different form.

Grounded in learning science

  • Bloom's 2-Sigma (1984): mastery learning plus tutoring, industrialized rather than idealized.
  • EEF Toolkit: feedback +6, mastery learning +5, one-to-one tuition +4 months of additional progress.
  • Spaced retrieval (Dunlosky et al., 2013): the basis of the D+2/4/8/16 schedule.
  • Knowledge graph: 180 nodes / 246 prerequisite edges (primary mathematics), 56 item templates, mappable to national curricula.
  • Weekly AI-attributed report: error causes, re-teach suggestions, next-week deliberate practice plan.
Privacy by construction: learner data lives on-device (JSON export), single offline deployment file — GDPR-friendly at the architecture level, not by policy statement.

08 · 安全合规

机器计数，教师定夺。

数据与 AI 治理

  • 本地化部署 / 私有云可选；家庭版端侧存储

  • 置信度标记，低置信题目转人工复核

08 · Trust & compliance

Machines count. Teachers decide.

Every AI output is a draft: scores are editable, comments overridable, and nothing is published until a teacher signs off. That is the design, not a disclaimer.

GDPR by design

  • Data minimisation, purpose limitation, defined retention schedules
  • On-prem or EU-resident deployment; learner edition fully on-device
  • Zero ad-tech, zero third-party trackers
  • Children's data safeguards; DPIA support, DPA and EU SCCs available
  • Encryption in transit and at rest; RBAC plus audit logs

EU AI Act readiness (education = high-risk)

  • Human oversight: grades remain drafts until a teacher signs off; full override at item level
  • Transparency: explainable per-question reports; no covert tracking of students
  • Record-keeping: audit logs plus a technical documentation pack for procurement
  • Data governance: student data never used to train models; confidence thresholds route uncertain items to teachers
Procurement reality: DPIA, DPA, SCCs and technical documentation are prepared before the first pilot, not after the first objection.

10 · 常见问题

使用与部署前，您可能想问

学生数据存在哪里？

学校版支持本地化部署或私有云；家庭 / 师生版数据完全存于本机（JSON 导出备份）。学生数据不用于模型训练，无第三方追踪。

需要答题卡或专用纸张吗？

不需要。直接批改教师日常 Word / PDF 文件与普通作业纸，不需要改版、不需要套印。

支持哪些教材版本？

内置人教版结构小学数学知识图谱（180 节点 / 246 先修边）及 56 个考点出题模板；支持按学校教材目录校准，多版本教材适配可选。

学校现有设备能否使用？

任意 TWAIN / 网络扫描仪或复合机均可经 SDK 接入；参考机型用于快速验证闭环，不必先换设备。

AI 批改的准确性如何保障？

批改基于评分规则并带置信度标记，低置信题目自动转人工复核；AI 结果均为草稿，教师终审签核——"机器计数，教师定夺"。

如何开始？

建议从 90 天试点开始：先在一个年级跑通闭环，输出基于 KPI 的效果报告，再确定扩大部署方案。

11 · FAQ

Questions we expect

Where is student data stored?

On-prem or EU-resident region for schools; the learner edition stores everything on-device with JSON export. Student data is never used to train models, and there are no third-party trackers.

Do we need special answer sheets or paper?

No. The system grades ordinary worksheets and works directly on Word/PDF originals — no reformatting, no overprinting.

Which languages and curricula do you support?

English first, German and French next, plus a curriculum-mapping service (England NC, Cambridge Primary, IB PYP; DACH and Nordic on request). Latin-script handwriting OCR is on the roadmap.

What hardware is required?

Any TWAIN or network scanner, MFP or copier through the capture SDK. Reference devices (SD-5501, S-6001) prove the loop; partner printers are the target platform.

How accurate is AI grading — and who is accountable?

Rubric-grounded scoring with confidence flags; low-confidence items queue for teacher review. Nothing becomes final without human sign-off, and every score is overridable.

How do we start?

A 90-day pilot in one year group: 3–5 classes, a KPI-based impact report, then a joint scale-up decision. Write to cr-solution@rz-info.cn to scope it.
